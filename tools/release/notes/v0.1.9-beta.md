A beta, mainly to try the first **macOS build**. Please report what works and what doesn't on your Mac.

## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.35+: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `cadrs-v0.1.9-beta-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86_64 (Windows 10 or 11) | `cadrs-v0.1.9-beta-x86_64-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel | `cadrs-v0.1.9-beta-universal-macos.dmg` |

The Linux and Windows archives hold the executable, its `libraries/` folder (the built-in symbols, footprints and 3D models), the README and the licenses. Nothing needs installing: unpack it and run `cadrs` (Linux) or `cadrs.exe` (Windows), keeping `libraries/` beside it. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the files' checksums.

On macOS, open the `.dmg` and drag cadrs to Applications. The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS won't open it: right-click cadrs in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (on macOS 15 and later, open it once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security). Updates on macOS are downloaded from the release page and dragged over the old app.

On Linux, the app needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver, and ALSA and udev (present on desktop distributions). The Windows build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Documents are saved in `~/.local/share/cadrs/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\cadrs\` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/cadrs/` on macOS.

## What's new since v0.1.8

- **macOS build (beta).** A universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs (#103). It hasn't been tried on many Macs yet.
- **Variable Studio.** Document-wide variable tables. Each row can use the rows above it and the tables inserted into the studio. Part Studios, other studios and assembly mate offsets can use them (#113).
- **Documents page** (#112):
  - Ctrl+click and Shift+click select many.
  - Drag documents and folders into folders, or onto the breadcrumb.
  - Folders nest.
  - Folders get a right-click menu: Rename, Copy (with what they hold), Move to, Move to trash, and Restore and Delete permanently in the Trash.
- **Surfaces built in pieces are one face** (#114). A torus, an ellipse's or a spline's ring, a sphere or a cone revolved or extruded in two features, mirrored, or patterned round a full turn, now joins into one face with no edge between the pieces.
- **Modelling** (from checking cadrs against [SolidCraft's](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft) 83 reference parts, which now all pass, #101):
  - A feature pattern of a cut copies the cut's whole tool, so overlapping patterned holes all cut (#104).
  - Inward shells have sharp cavity corners, as in Onshape (#105).
  - A sweep along lines and arcs gets a face per piece of the path (#107).
  - Four or more fillets meeting at a point make a sphere corner where they can.
  - A variable fillet's radius can go to zero at a vertex.
  - A curve pattern takes a spacing typed rounded.
  - Sheet metal flanges can give a corner gap, and a miter angle with Outer alignment no longer makes the walls overlap.
- **Chamfers** run along tangent chains past smooth edges and round fillets narrower than the chamfer (#85).
- **BREP import** (#99).
- **Speed:**
  - Faster feature edits and tab switches in large documents.
  - Smooth instance drags in large assemblies (#71).
  - Large sketch dimension changes solve in steps (#93).
