7.3    Preventing tolerance growth

     Compression    normally results in some growth of local
tolerances. Ideally models defined to a certain tolerance should
unite to create models of the same or similar tolerance. Although
this seems not to be achievable in all cases, tolerance growth
should be contained where possible.

8.    CONCLUSION

     Local tolerances on a b-rep model can be used as a foundation
for robust solid modelling and reliable model transfer between
different systems. This technique has been implemented in a
widely used production solid modeller, and has been used to solve
real modelling problems.
