           A multi-sided generalization of the C0 Coons patch

                                                           Péter Salvi

                                         Budapest University of Technology and Economics




      Abstract
      Most multi-sided transfinite surfaces require cross-derivatives at the boundaries. Here we show a general n-sided
      patch that interpolates all boundaries based on only positional information. The surface is a weighted sum of n
      Coons patches, using a parameterization based on Wachspress coordinates.
